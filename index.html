How FitTrack calculates your targets

``` Calories use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with a moderate activity multiplier of 1.55. ```

``` Protein is calculated at approximately 2 g/kg body weight. ```

``` Fat is calculated at approximately 0.8 g/kg body weight. ```

``` Remaining calories are assigned to carbohydrates. ```

``` Calories burned are estimated from your recorded workouts and walking steps. ```
